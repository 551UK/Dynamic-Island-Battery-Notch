# Dynamic Island Battery Notch

Battery percentage outline for **iPhone 14 Pro Max** on **iOS 16 (Dopamine rootless)**.

Dynamic Island Battery Notch displays your remaining charge as a coloured line around the Dynamic Island. As your battery drains, the line gets shorter, so you can see how much battery is left at a glance.

- The outline shrinks as the battery drains, with automatic red/yellow/green or custom colours.
- Lock Screen padlock matches the battery percentage colour theme, including charging.
- Custom charging colour, optional pulsing, and separate normal/charging line thickness.
- Fits screen recording and the Lock Screen; hides during calls.
